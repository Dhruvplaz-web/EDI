## 2. Research Purpose & Investigative Nature

You are invited to participate in a clinical research study. **This software module is in Research Mode and is designated for Research Use Only (RUO).**

- **What is being studied?** During your routine tele-psychiatric consultation, the research software will analyze non-verbal communication cues:
  1. *Vocal Acoustic Telemetry:* Fundamental frequency (pitch), speech pauses, jitter, and shimmer.
  2. *Facial Kinematic Action Units:* Micro-movements of facial landmarks (e.g., brow furrowing AU4, lip corner depression AU15).
- **Not a Diagnostic Tool:** The software does **not** diagnose depression, bipolar disorder, or any psychiatric illness. The generated numbers are research metrics under scientific investigation.
- **Clinical Separation:** Your doctor will make all clinical diagnoses, prescriptions, and treatment recommendations completely independently of the research software. Biomarker scores are excluded from your official clinical record unless the doctor explicitly archives an investigative research note.

## 4. Data Privacy, Storage, and Security (§ 8 DPDP Act)

- **Pseudonymization:** Your audio and video streams are analyzed in real-time. Raw video recordings are not retained unless separate recording consent was granted. Biomarker metrics are decoupled from your direct name and tagged with an anonymized Research Subject ID.
- **Data Localization:** All research data is stored exclusively in **Google Cloud India regions (`asia-south1` Mumbai)**.
- **No Third-Party Commercial Sale:** Research data will never be sold, leased, or utilized for commercial targeted advertising or credit scoring.

## 5. Potential Benefits & Risks

- **Direct Benefits:** There is no guaranteed direct therapeutic benefit to you. The research aims to improve scientific understanding of computer-assisted documentation in Indian healthcare settings.
- **Potential Risks:** There are no physical risks. Potential privacy risks are mitigated through end-to-end encryption, strict role-based access controls, and adherence to DPDP Act safeguards.
