## 3. Itemized Purposes of Processing (§ 5 & 6 DPDP Act)

Your personal data is processed solely for specified, clear, and unbundled purposes:

1. **Telemedicine Consultations:** Facilitating live WebRTC video and audio consultations with your registered psychiatrist or medical practitioner.
2. **Clinical Record Keeping & E-Prescriptions:** Enabling your doctor to document consultations, write compliant prescriptions, and maintain your official medical record for at least three (3) years as mandated by the NMC Telemedicine Practice Guidelines 2020.
3. **Transactional Notifications:** Sending OTP codes, appointment confirmations, and secure consultation links via DLT-registered SMS or email. **Zero clinical details or psychiatric diagnoses are ever included in SMS/WhatsApp messages.**
4. **Emergency Escalation:** In the event of acute distress, providing direct access to verified national mental health helplines (Tele-MANAS `14416`, KIRAN `1800-599-0019`, ERSS `112`).
5. **Research Analysis (RUO):** If and only if your clinic has recorded institutional ethics approval and you grant explicit research consent, anonymized vocal/facial kinematics may be evaluated for investigative purposes.

## 4. Consent & Right to Withdraw (§ 6(4) DPDP Act)

- Your consent is requested separately for each discrete processing purpose.
- You have the absolute statutory right to **withdraw your consent at any time** through your clinic portal settings or by informing your clinician during a call.
- Withdrawal of consent takes effect immediately and halts related non-mandatory processing.
- *Legal Limitation:* Under the National Medical Commission Telemedicine Guidelines 2020, medical consultation records and prescriptions already created cannot be immediately erased upon consent withdrawal and must be preserved for the statutory 3-year medical record period.

## 5. Children & Minors (< 18 Years) (§ 9 DPDP Act)

- We do not knowingly offer direct services to children under the age of 18 without verifiable guardian consent.
- For minor patients, a verified parent or legal guardian must link their profile and provide authorization.
- **Strict Prohibition:** All behavioral tracking, profiling, vocal pitch analysis, and facial biomarker evaluation are strictly disabled for minors under Section 9(3) of the DPDP Act.

## 6. Data Residency, Security & Storage (§ 8 DPDP Act)

- **100% India Cloud Residency:** All health records, consultation data, audit logs, and backups are stored exclusively within Google Cloud regions in India (`asia-south1` Mumbai and `asia-south2` Delhi).
- **Encryption:** All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3. All data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 with Google Cloud Key Management Service.
- **Immutable Audit Logging:** Every access to your health record is recorded in an append-only, tamper-evident audit log maintained for regulatory accountability.

## 7. Data Principal Rights (§ 11, 12, 13, 14 DPDP Act)

As a Data Principal, you are entitled to:
1. **Right to Access & Summary:** Request a summary of your personal data processed by the clinic.
2. **Right to Correction & Updating:** Request correction of inaccurate or outdated medical intake or identity details.
3. **Right to Erasure:** Request deletion of your personal data (subject to statutory medical retention holds).
4. **Right to Grievance Redressal:** Lodge a complaint with our Data Protection Officer / Grievance Officer.
5. **Right to Nominate:** Nominate an individual who shall exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity (§ 14 DPDP Act / § 14 Mental Healthcare Act 2017).
