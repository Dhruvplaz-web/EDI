## 1. Onboarding Covenant & Eligibility

This Onboarding Agreement confirms the enrollment of **[Insert Healthcare Institution Name]** (**"Clinic"**) into the Praxis AI clinical software network.

To activate clinical operations, the Clinic covenants and warrants:
1. **Legal Entity Status:** The Clinic is a duly registered clinical establishment under the Clinical Establishments (Registration and Regulation) Act, 2010 or relevant State legislation.
2. **Designated Clinic Administrator:** The Clinic has appointed a verified Medical Superintendent or Administrative Officer authorized to manage tenant accounts.
3. **Primary Data Fiduciary Undertaking:** The Clinic accepts its role as the statutory Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act 2023, responsible for lawful notice, valid consent collection, and patient grievance redressal.

---

## 2. Mandatory Practitioner Verification Process

Prior to conducting consultations, each practitioner provisioned on the platform must submit:
1. Full Legal Name (matching official medical registry).
2. Qualifying Medical Degree (e.g., MBBS, MD Psychiatry, DPM).
3. Medical Registration Number (NMC or State Medical Council) and Registration Year.
4. For Clinical Psychologists: Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI) CRR Number.

**Verification Rule:** Unverified practitioners remain in a sandboxed, restricted state and cannot initiate consultations or generate prescriptions until Praxis AI admin verification against the NMC/SMC registry is recorded.

---

## 3. Prescribing Rights & Psychologist Restrictions

The Clinic acknowledges and enforces the following platform restrictions:
1. **Prescription Authority:** Only Verified Registered Medical Practitioners (Psychiatrists) may generate e-prescriptions.
2. **Clinical Psychologist Restriction:** In compliance with the Mental Healthcare Act 2017 and Indian Medical Council regulations, clinical psychologists are restricted by server-side permissions from generating, signing, or issuing medical prescriptions.
3. **Restricted Drugs:** The issuance of Schedule X drugs and NDPS substances is blocked on the platform.

---

## 4. Financial Terms, GST, and Invoicing

1. **Subscription Invoicing:** Invoices are billed in Indian Rupees (INR) at the beginning of each billing cycle (monthly or annual).
2. **GST Compliance:** Invoices carry **18% GST** under **SAC 998313**. The Clinic shall furnish its 15-character GSTIN to enable input tax credit (ITC) reconciliation via GSTR-2B.
3. **TDS Certificates:** Where TDS is withheld under Section 194J, quarterly TDS certificates (Form 16A) must be transmitted to `billing@praxis-ai.in`.
