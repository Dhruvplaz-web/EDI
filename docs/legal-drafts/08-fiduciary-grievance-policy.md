## 1. Statutory Right to Grievance Redressal

Under Section 13 of the DPDP Act, every Data Principal (patient, guardian, or nominated representative) has the inviolable statutory right to readily available means of grievance redressal provided by the Data Fiduciary and its Data Processor in respect of:
1. Any act or omission by the Clinic or Praxis AI regarding personal data obligations.
2. The exercise of statutory rights (access, correction, erasure, nomination).
3. The withdrawal or scope of consent.
4. Security safeguards, suspected data compromises, or unauthorized disclosures.

1. **In-App Portal:** Navigating to `Settings > Privacy & DPDP Rights > File Grievance`.
2. **Direct Email:** Sending an email from the registered email address to `grievance-officer@praxis-ai.in` with the subject line *"Grievance under DPDP Act 2023"*.

## 5. Escalation to the Data Protection Board of India (§ 13(3) DPDP Act)

If the Data Principal:
1. Does not receive a response from the Grievance Officer within thirty (30) days; or
2. Is aggrieved by the substantive decision or remediation provided by the Grievance Officer;

The Data Principal may submit a formal complaint to the **Data Protection Board of India (DPBI)** through the official digital portal at `https://dpbi.gov.in` within sixty (60) days of the grievance decision.
