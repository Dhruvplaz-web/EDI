## 2. CDSCO MDSW Feature-by-Feature Risk Analysis

Under the Medical Devices Rules, 2017 and **CDSCO Guidance Document on Medical Device Software (CDSCO/MD/GD/MDSW/01/2026)**:

| Feature Name | Function & Clinical Role | Medical Device Software (SaMD)? | Likely CDSCO Risk Class | Regulatory Pathway / Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Video / Audio Teleconsultation** | Real-time WebRTC audio/video call between RMP and patient | **NO** (General telecommunications / administrative) | **Excluded** | Standard Telemedicine Practice Guidelines compliance; no CDSCO license required. |
| **Patient Intake & Scheduling** | Digital demographic collection and appointment calendar | **NO** (Administrative practice management) | **Excluded** | General IT Act & DPDP compliance; no CDSCO license required. |
| **Clinician E-Prescription Writer** | Records medication orders, checks drug schedule restrictions, generates PDF | **NO** (Digital document generation tool; clinician holds clinical judgment) | **Excluded** | Follows NMC Telemedicine prescription format. Does not auto-generate drug choices. |
| **Automated Clinical SOAP Note Formulation** | LLM-generated note synthesis from session transcript and observations | **POTENTIALLY YES** (Clinical decision support) | **Class B** (Inform clinical management) | **DISABLED BY DEFAULT**. Gated behind "Research Mode" with recorded Institutional Ethics Committee approval. Clinician review mandatory. |
| **Vocal Acoustic Biomarkers (Jitter / Shimmer / F0)** | Bio-acoustic feature extraction from microphone stream | **YES** (Derives physiological markers intended to reflect distress) | **Class B / Class C** (if used for psychiatric diagnosis) | **DISABLED BY DEFAULT**. Gated behind Research Mode. Labeled: *"Research use only. Not for clinical diagnosis or treatment decisions."* |
| **Facial Kinematic Action Units (AU4 / AU15)** | MediaPipe facial landmark mesh to compute brow lowerer and lip depressor activity | **YES** (Physiological kinematic inference) | **Class B / Class C** | **DISABLED BY DEFAULT**. Gated behind Research Mode. Data not committed to official EMR. |
| **Distress Discrepancy Alert $D(t)$** | Assistive alert when verbal sentiment diverges from involuntary involuntary nonverbal signals | **YES** (Active diagnostic anomaly detection) | **Class B / Class C** | **DISABLED BY DEFAULT**. Gated behind Research Mode. |
| **Crisis Keyword Detection (Tele-MANAS/Emergency)** | Assistive keyword scanner identifying high-risk ideation in text/transcript | **NO / LOW RISK** (Triage assistive safety net) | **Class A (if classified)** | Labeled as non-diagnostic safety escalation helper; provides emergency numbers (112, Tele-MANAS 14416). |
